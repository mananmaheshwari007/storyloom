The story

Writing & illustrating

You don't write — you remember. Share memories in any form: a rambling call, voice notes, old photos, bullet points at midnight. Our writers turn them into a story, and you approve every word before it's illustrated.

From your photos. We ask for a handful of clear pictures of each person and any places that matter. You approve the character likenesses early — before the full spreads are painted — so the people in the book are unmistakably yours.

Yes — anyone and anything that belongs in your story. The dog, the grandparents, the scooter that never started, the exact terrace with the exact water tank. Specificity is the whole point.

English and Hindi are our home ground, and we can craft books in other Indian languages with a native-speaking writer — ask us when you begin. Bilingual books (one language per page) are a beautiful option for grandparents.

Almost every Storyloom is a surprise. We coordinate entirely with you, and delivery is timed and addressed however secrecy requires — including delivery to a neighbour, an office, or a co-conspirator.

The process

Reviews, revisions & timelines

Three to five weeks from first conversation to doorstep, depending on length and season. If you have a fixed date — an anniversary, a Diwali homecoming — tell us on day one and we plan the whole book backwards from it.

Delivery & care

Shipping, refunds & the fine print

Yes — every Storyloom ships in a rigid, protective keepsake box by tracked courier to any pincode in India. Delivery within India is included in every edition.

Yes to both. Many Storylooms are commissioned by family abroad and delivered to parents in India — or made in India and shipped to Toronto, Dubai, or London. Everything happens over WhatsApp and email, in any timezone.

Because every book is a one-of-one commission, we can't resell a cancelled Storyloom — so refunds are staged: full refund before writing begins, partial once writing or illustration is underway. If a book ever arrives damaged, we reprint and reship it at our cost, no questions asked.

Your story stays yours, always. We only ever share a book — in our library or on our pages — with the family's explicit permission. Most families say yes proudly; saying no changes nothing about your experience.

It shouldn't be. It should be read — at bedtimes, on anniversaries, on ordinary Tuesdays. The paper and binding are chosen to survive affection. The keepsake box is there for the years between readings.
